## Design context

Human-paced technical editorial. Sharp borders, warm paper-like surfaces, serif display headlines, sans-serif body copy and monospaced annotations make the studio feel authored rather than automated. Alejandro + Fede appear as a compact capability signature, never as a personal portfolio.

## Direction narrative

The page starts with a direct question—what is blocking the business?—then lets each audience choose a doorway. Concrete seller, developer and Excel problems are explained conversationally before the deterministic/LLM boundary, coexistence model, WhatsApp moments and a large QR invitation.

## Strengths

- Most approachable and differentiated for live human conversations at the event.
- Strongest Excel/PymIA explanation and seller pain language.
- Premium but less corporate; supports Alejandro + Fede credibility without biography overload.
- Mobile stacking and QR interaction are naturally legible.

## Risks

- Serif/editorial tone must retain enough technical authority for developers.
- Warm surfaces can soften contrast if implementation tokens are not audited.
- Local logo asset must replace Stitch's placeholder at build time: `E:/BuenosPasos/smartbridge/Meli2026/landing/logopymia2.jpg`.

## Animation concept

Human-paced hero reveal, vertical scroll reveals, moderate stagger, line annotations, small hover emphasis and CTA feedback. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with opacity-only fallback.
