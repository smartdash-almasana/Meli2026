## Recommendation

**RECOMMENDED_DIRECTION: C — Human Technical Studio**

It offers the best balance of human clarity, concrete seller/Excel problems, technical credibility and mobile/event usability. It makes the conversation feel real while preserving the deterministic-software boundary. Direction B is the strongest alternative when the audience is mostly technical; Direction A is the strongest premium editorial fallback.

## Tradeoffs

- **A:** Highest editorial authority; needs extra concrete proof points for sellers.
- **B:** Best systems explanation; needs restraint to avoid a cyber/DevOps look.
- **C:** Best mixed-audience comprehension; needs contrast and technical density preserved in implementation.

## Shared requirements verified in all prompts

- Exact hero promise, subheadline and primary CTA.
- Three visible audience doorways.
- Seller, developer and Excel/PymIA sections.
- Deterministic + LLM responsibility split.
- Integration before migration and coexistence map.
- WhatsApp operational use cases without a generic chatbot.
- Dominant `QR → SURVEY` event CTA with short-URL placeholder.
- Subtle animation concepts with `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
