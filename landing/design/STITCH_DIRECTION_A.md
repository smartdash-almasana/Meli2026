## Design context

Typography-led editorial technology studio. Asymmetric 12-column composition, oversized statements, thin structural rules, annotated diagrams and generous negative space. The narrative moves from integration-over-migration to audience-specific problems and ends with a QR-led event CTA. The design system is flat, high-contrast and deliberately avoids SaaS decoration.

## Direction narrative

PymIA appears as a precise technical studio that adds the missing piece to an existing operation. Sellers, developers/integrators and Excel-heavy businesses enter through three visible doorways before the page explains deterministic software, LLM assistance, coexistence with current systems and WhatsApp alerts.

## Strengths

- Strongest editorial/premium impression and clear hierarchy in the first 15 seconds.
- Asymmetry and typographic scale create a distinctive event presence.
- Excellent QR ending and simple diagrams that can be implemented without illustration assets.
- Keeps PymIA positioned as an integrator, not a replacement suite.

## Risks

- Large type and long vertical rhythm need careful mobile tuning.
- Editorial abstraction may require stronger seller proof points in implementation.
- Local logo asset must replace Stitch's textual logo placeholder at build time: `E:/BuenosPasos/smartbridge/Meli2026/landing/logopymia2.jpg`.

## Animation concept

Hero reveal, scroll-triggered section reveal, restrained stagger for numbered lists, line-drawing annotations, subtle hover emphasis and CTA feedback. Disable transforms and continuous motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
