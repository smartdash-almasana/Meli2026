## Design context

An operational map is the main visual metaphor. Mercado Libre, Excel, ERP, Legacy, Suites, WhatsApp and PymIA are connected by nodes, lines and controlled handoffs. The page uses technical precision, system status cues and a large event QR panel while keeping the integration—not migration—message explicit.

## Direction narrative

The attendee sees their current stack first, then understands exactly where PymIA can connect: seller operations, developer capabilities, Excel diagnostics, deterministic rules plus LLM interpretation, and actionable WhatsApp alerts.

## Strengths

- Clearest explanation of integration and coexistence for a mixed technical audience.
- Strong developer/integrator story through APIs, modules, white-label and automation nodes.
- System map makes the “missing piece” message memorable from a distance.
- QR panel and mobile reflow are explicit in the generated concept.

## Risks

- Dark technical styling can drift toward cyber/DevOps or generic AI aesthetics if not restrained.
- Node maps may become dense on mobile; preserve a single reading path.
- Local logo asset must replace Stitch's placeholder at build time: `E:/BuenosPasos/smartbridge/Meli2026/landing/logopymia2.jpg`.

## Animation concept

Node-and-line drawing on section entry, subtle grid/status reveal, moderate stagger, hover emphasis and CTA feedback. Keep motion finite and disable heavy effects with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
